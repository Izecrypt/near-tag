How it works in 3 steps

No trading charts to stare at. You get a daily job, and the job pays.

1

Tag it

Post on X with $nTAG and @its_izecube. Sign in with X once to bind your wallet — your address never goes in a post.

2

Hold it

Your points grow with the average amount you hold across the whole day. Buying right before the deadline doesn't help.

3

Claim it

Each day, validators check the tags and the contract works out your share. Press claim and 95% lands in your wallet.

Two ways to earn

Use one, or both. Together they add up.

Tagging on X

Posts that get likes, replies, reposts, and quotes score higher.

  • Original posts count the most
  • Daily limits stop spam, so good posts beat many posts
  • One X account per wallet. The first link wins.

Holding

Earn 0.5 points for every whole token you hold per day.

  • Balances sampled about every 10 minutes
  • Your day is the average, not a single snapshot
  • Late buyers can't grab a big share and leave

You can never earn more in a day than you hold.
