the loop

  1. tag. post on X with the token cashtag, the token's X account, and your wallet address in the text. the attestor scores the engagement.
  2. hold. your time-weighted average balance across the day also earns. buying at the last second does not farm it.
  3. validate. holders above the threshold vote on each tag on-chain. confirmed tags pay; fabricated tags burn validator credibility for nothing.
  4. settle. once a day the attestor submits the signed epoch. the pot is at most 10% of the treasury, and nobody can earn more than they held that day.
  5. claim. rewards are pulled, not pushed. every claim takes a 5% skim back to the treasury. every reward feeds the pool.

honesty limits

  • oracle. one Ed25519 attestor signs each daily epoch, bound to this contract and chain ID. the contract enforces caps, dedupe, quorum, pot limits and the holding ceiling. it cannot independently verify X engagement; validator votes exist to discipline that.
  • demo status. the hackathon demo epoch is labeled synthetic and runs on NEAR testnet. live mode is the same code, same rules.
  • sybil limits. a wallet's first attested X link is permanent. the contract does not prove real-world identity; fingerprints can only ever be paid once.
  • buyback. a NEAR pot is reserved for market buybacks. the refill() swap is disabled until the testnet router and pool path are verified. no buyback is claimed as executed.
  • the token itself. at launch, TAG is a factory-standard NEP-141 from meme.cooking: no mint, no transfer fee inside the token, no admin powers over it, ever. the 5% skim and the treasury live in this separate mechanics contract.
